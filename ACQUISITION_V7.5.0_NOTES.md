# Adam Acquisition v7.5.0 — Calling & Invitations

This release adds a governed Calling & Invitations module to Adam's main navigation.

- Prepare and review Outlook meeting invitations from saved contacts.
- Real Outlook invitation creation remains behind explicit Owner Approval and the existing Microsoft connector.
- Prepare phone call launches from saved contacts.
- Phone calls open the device dialer only after explicit Owner Approval.
- No autonomous PSTN/VoIP voice-call capability is claimed in this release.
- Future real AI telephony must use an approved voice connector and retain Adam AI identity disclosure and owner policy controls.
- No credentials, private contact data, or runtime state are added to the buyer package.
