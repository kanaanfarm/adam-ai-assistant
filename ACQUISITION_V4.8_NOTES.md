# Adam Acquisition v4.8.0 — Post-Meeting Cross-App Follow-Up

Adds a buyer-safe governed foundation that converts structured meeting outcomes into follow-up preparation/execution across email, calendar, documents and WhatsApp.

- Preview before execution.
- Consequential actions require explicit Owner Approval at the exact action.
- Connector transports are injected; credentials are not owned by the core.
- Buyer evidence exposes receipts/counts, not message bodies, contact values or meeting-memory contents.
- Synthetic self-test performs no external network action and sends no real email/calendar invite.
