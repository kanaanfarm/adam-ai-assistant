# Adam Acquisition v8.3.1 — Multilingual Professional Meeting Representative

Focused upgrade from v8.3.0.5.

- Meeting understanding defaults to **Auto — All languages** with multilingual server transcription and code-switching.
- Adam's **reply language defaults to English**, regardless of the participant's input language.
- A participant can explicitly ask Adam to speak/answer in Arabic or another supported language. Arabic maps to natural Lebanese Arabic (`ar-LB`). The explicit choice remains active for the current meeting session until another explicit switch.
- Adds a **cross-disciplinary professional** scope with automatic discipline inference and optional discipline selector.
- Professional reasoning is bounded: Adam distinguishes general knowledge from project-specific facts and does not claim licenses or unsupported authority.
- Owner briefing, AI identity disclosure, non-impersonation, and consequential-action approval boundaries remain preserved.
- Real Teams/Zoom/Google Meet autonomous join is still not claimed; a real approved platform adapter remains a separate integration step.
